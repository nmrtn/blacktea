Spending controls for AI agents

Your agent can pay. You decide how much.

Black Tea sits between an AI agent and its wallet. A policy file sets the limits, a human approves what falls outside them, and every approval leaves a receipt. Open source. SDK, CLI and MCP server.

What it stops

Three things an agent with a wallet gets wrong, or gets talked into.

01

The price changes after you said yes.

An approval is bound to the exact request. A higher amount, another payee or another network at settle time is refused before anything is signed.

02

Many small payments add up to a large one.

would_spend counts what was already paid, per wallet or per host, before it allows the next one.

03

The same approval is used twice.

A held payment can be completed once. A replayed, altered or expired approval never reaches the wallet.
