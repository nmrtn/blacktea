# Brand changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-28

First kit, from one brand-kit session in normal mode.

- Territory: dark only, near-black with one amber. Chosen by picking between real sites (Linear, Stripe, Resend, Oxide and Railway over PostHog, htmx, Mercury, Tailscale and Vercel; neither of Charm and PlanetScale).
- Type: Geist with Geist Mono. Serif refused outright.
- Mark: a cup of black tea seen from above. Chosen over a receipt symbol.
- Device: the receipt, with dotted leaders between every key and value.
- Concept: the moment before the money moves.
- Refusals recorded: sign-up walls and waitlists; mascots, illustrations and stock photos.
- Not built: the brand guidelines site (`brand/site/`). Voice and positioning are assumptions until checked against launch feedback (see `provenance` in `tokens.json`).
